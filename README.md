### 🧠 Engineering Mastery & Mindset
I am a highly driven **Platform & Systems Engineer**, acting as a **Tech Lead** and the core technical reference for my team. I am a specialist in **Java and Spring Boot** 🍃, and a passionate architect of resilient solutions. I thrive on mastering complex **System Designs** 🏗️ and building infrastructures that scale flawlessly.

My approach to engineering—and to life—is deeply rooted in the concept of being **Antifragile** 🛡️ (by Nassim Taleb). I don't just aim to withstand chaos, stress, or uncertainty; I design systems, craft code, and forge habits to learn, adapt, and grow exceptionally stronger because of them.

*   **⚙️ Backend & APIs:** Spring Boot, NestJS, Serverless Architecture
*   **🏛️ System Architecture:** Advanced System Design, highly scalable and distributed systems
*   **📱 Mobile Development:** React Native, Expo
*   **☁️ Cloud, DevOps & Automation:** AWS, GitHub Actions (CI/CD)
